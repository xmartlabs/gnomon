# GStack scorecard

<p class="gn-thesis">The GStack scorecard is a second, separate reading: how a developer builds, scored on three axes from 0 to 10. It sits next to the AQ and does not feed into it.</p>

Where the AQ asks how well someone operates agents, the scorecard looks at the build itself: output and delegation, planning and reasoning, and engineering hygiene. The same rule applies here: a signal a source cannot record is left out, not scored as zero.
